GETTING STARTED
Run a SAP-to-robot process step by step

      
    
        
  1. 1. Load the SAP EWM example.Load Example opens the complete Order-to-Dispatch model: SAP context, Joule orchestration, AMRs, picking, conveyor, verification, and goods issue.
    2. 
        
  2. 2. Enter the 3D Twin.Switch to 3D Twin and orbit the connected SAP and physical zones. Click any mesh to open its cockpit or implementation environment.
    3. 
        
  3. 3. Inspect business context.Open SAP LoB Source, SAP BDC Cockpit, and SAP Data Product to inspect orders, SQL, PAL HGBT, graphs, contracts, and lineage.
    4. 
        
  4. 4. Direct the outcome with Joule.Open Joule Agents, enter a bounded warehouse outcome, and delegate. The trace separates assistant intent, domain reasoning, robot skill, and governance review.
    5. 
        
  5. 5. Program the physical routine.In Embodied AI Lab select SAP EWM Multi-Robot Order Fulfillment. Inspect GRAFCET, I/O bindings, routine code, and commissioning steps.
    6. 
        
  6. 6. Simulate before commanding.Run the routine in Simulation, advance individual GRAFCET steps, then compare Fast DES, Real-time, and Monte Carlo metrics.
    7. 
        
  7. 7. Validate integrations.Use the SAP BTP runway to dry-run OData, REST, MQTT, OPC UA, ROS-adjacent, database, socket, and file adapters without production writes.
    8. 
        
  8. 8. Test governance.Open Governance and Human Approval Gate. Shadow may read signals; assisted mode requires approval; live robot commands remain locked.
    9. 
        
  9. 9. Close the loop.Inspect SAP HANA Cloud data engines, review evidence, and save a reproducible snapshot containing business, agent, robot, and policy traces.
